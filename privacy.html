Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

The short version: POOL.EXE collects nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers, and the app never connects to the internet. Everything the game remembers stays on your iPhone.

Who makes the app

POOL.EXE is made by Mihhail Kornõšev, an individual developer based in Estonia ("I", "me"). If you have any questions about this policy, email marjamaa14@gmail.com.

Data I collect

None. The app doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, it has no sign-in, and it makes no network requests. It doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or anything else on your phone.

What's stored on your iPhone

To work properly, the game saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your settings (level, cloth colour, controls, sound, vibration and the like)
  • a game in progress, so you can resume it
  • your best score for each level
  • small markers, such as whether you've seen the tips or the boot screen, and whether you've unlocked the VGA table

This information never leaves your phone and I can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it. Like any app's data, it may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backups. Those backups are managed by Apple under your own settings.

Apple

You download POOL.EXE through Apple's App Store, which handles that process under Apple's privacy policy. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you, and I use them only to fix problems. If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may share your name, your email address and any feedback or crash reports you send with me, as part of how TestFlight works. I use them only to improve the app.

If you email me

If you contact me for support, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you, I don't share it with anyone, and I'll delete it if you ask.

Children

POOL.EXE is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data, it collects no information from children either.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since the app collects nothing, the only personal data I could hold is an email you've sent me. Write to me and I'll show it to you or delete it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Estonia, that's the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (Data Protection Inspectorate).

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if I add Game Center leaderboards), I'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
