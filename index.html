How to play

Aim

Drag anywhere around the cue ball to turn the cue. Tap a ball to aim straight at it. The two arrow buttons on the right rail nudge the aim a fraction at a time. If the cue turns too fast or too slowly for you, change it in Settings → Aim speed.

Shoot

Pull the cue on the right rail down and let go to strike. The further you pull, the harder the shot. Push it back up to call the shot off.

Spin

Tap the little cue ball on the right rail. Hit low for draw, high for follow, and left or right for side spin (English).

Left-handed? Settings → Controls → Left hand moves all the shot controls to the left rail.

Score

Every ball you pot scores points, with extra for banks, combinations and long pots. Pot several in a row and a multiplier kicks in. A foul wipes the points for that shot.

Rules

Standard eight-ball: pot all your solids or stripes, then the 8. Sink the 8 early, or foul on it, and you lose the game. A foul (scratching, hitting the wrong ball first, missing everything, or no ball touching a rail after contact) gives your opponent ball in hand anywhere on the table. After a scratch on the break, the cue ball goes behind the head string instead.

On the break, if you pot only solids or only stripes, that group is yours. Pot both and the table stays open. Settings → Who breaks lets you break every game, take turns, or let the winner break.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, nothing to unlock with money.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game never connects to the internet. It works on a plane, underground, anywhere.

Can I skip the boot screen?

Tap once to skip ahead, then tap again to start. To turn it off completely, open the menu → Settings → Boot screen → Off.

Can I turn off the computer jokes?

Yes: menu → Settings → Computer jokes → Off. Fouls then appear in plain wording, and losing on the 8 skips the blue screen.

Can I make the vibration softer, or turn it off?

Menu → Settings → Vibration. Subtle (the default) buzzes only for the big moments, Full adds a tick for every clack, rail and button, and Off turns it off.

I'm left-handed.

Menu → Settings → Controls → Left hand. The spin, fine aim and power cue move to the left rail.

The game is too easy, or too hard.

Choose a level on the main menu. Amateur shows the full aiming guide and gives you a forgiving opponent. Pro shows only the aim line, and the computer rarely misses.

There's no sound.

Check that Sound is On in Settings, that the volume is up, and that your iPhone isn't in silent mode. If it still happens, please email me with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The table always stands upright, in portrait.

Will I lose a game in progress if I close the app?

No. The current game is saved on your phone and you can resume it from the menu.

How do I reset my best scores?

Scores are stored only on your phone. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears them, along with your settings.

What's the VGA table?

A retro look for the table: flat colours, chunky pixels and CRT scanlines, like a game from the nineties. It's listed under Settings → Table style from the start, and it unlocks when you score over 1,000 points in one game. After that you can switch between Classic and VGA any time.
